# Claim Lifecycle

Echo tracks crypto narratives, so each claim should move through a small set of states instead of living forever as an unstructured text fragment.

## States

- New: a claim appears but has not been reinforced.
- Reinforced: separate actors repeat it without clear copy behavior.
- Contested: credible pushback or conflicting evidence appears.
- Durable: the claim persists across multiple cycles and still shapes behavior.
- Retired: the market stopped caring or the claim was resolved.

## Promote carefully

- Promotion should require time separation, not just volume.
- Contradiction should reduce confidence faster than repetition increases it.
- A durable claim still needs provenance so later summaries remain auditable.
